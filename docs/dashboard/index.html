WAPE · pooled over all quarter-hours; "Visible range" follows the zoom of the chart above

Setup. Zero-shot (no fine-tuning), CPU inference. One forecast per day, issued at 18:00 Europe/Brussels from data up to 17:45, for the next local calendar day (92/96/100 quarter-hours on DST days); every day of 2025 (365 forecasts). Each model is shown in its best configuration on 2025 (covariates and context length in its label), which makes its WAPE slightly optimistic. Lines are the median (P50) forecast; bands are P10-P90.

Contamination caveat. Timestral and t0-gamma weights were released on 2026-10-15, after the 2025 test year, and their real training data is not disclosed, so 2025 Elia data may have been seen in training. Chronos-2's weights date from 2025-10-30 and its disclosed training data lists no Belgian load.

Data revisions. Models read Elia's revised historical load as context; at 18:00 on each real day Elia's forecasters had only preliminary measurements and their own weather forecasts, which likely favours the models slightly.
