Setup. Zero-shot (no fine-tuning), CPU inference. One forecast per day, issued at 18:00 Europe/Brussels from data up to 17:45, for the next local calendar day (92/96/100 quarter-hours on DST days); every day of 2025 (365 forecasts). Lines are the median (P50) forecast; bands are P10-P90.

† Contamination caveat. Timestral and t0-gamma weights were released on 2026-10-15, after the 2025 test year, and their real training data is not disclosed, so 2025 Elia data may have been seen in training. Chronos-2's weights date from 2025-10-30 and its disclosed training data lists no Belgian load.

Data revisions. Models read the historical load as published at download time (2026-10-19). At 18:00 on each real day, Elia's forecasters only had the data available then. Elia can update measured values after the fact; whether the 2025 values were revised is not known here. If they were, the models may have had slightly cleaner history than Elia did.
